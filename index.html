About Preparics

Eight years.
One mission.

Preparics was founded in Varanasi in 2017 to give every student from this city the same quality of preparation available in Kota — without leaving home.

Small batches. Experienced faculty. An AI-powered practice platform that never runs out of questions. This is how we prepare students who compete at the national level.
